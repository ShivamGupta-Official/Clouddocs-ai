Enterprise Datacenter Environmental Guidelines

Section 1: Temperature and Humidity
All tier-3 datacenter server rooms must maintain an ambient inlet temperature between 18 degrees Celsius and 22 degrees Celsius (64.4°F to 71.6°F) to ensure optimal hardware longevity. Relative humidity should be kept strictly between 40% and 55% non-condensing to prevent static discharge and condensation.

Section 3: Power Distribution and Redundancy
Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) battery banks are calibrated to supply N+1 redundancy, providing at least 15 minutes of continuous full-load power while diesel backup generators initiate and synchronize with the automatic transfer switch (ATS).
